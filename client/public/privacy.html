Scheduly

Privacy notice

Updated 8 September 2026

Scheduly is operated by DarkMatter PTY LTD for its team and authorised client social accounts. It supports content planning, review, publishing and reporting. It is currently a hosted preview; live publishing is not enabled.

Information we process

We process work email addresses, assigned roles and client access, uploaded media, post copy, proposed publication times and records of content changes and approvals. When social account connections are enabled and authorised, we will also process the account identifiers, access credentials and platform data needed for the permissions you grant, including publication results and reporting metrics.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate team members, restrict client access, manage content and approvals, provide authorised publishing and reporting, and investigate errors. Platform data is not sold or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Service providers and access

Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and backend hosting. Vercel hosts the web application. Authorised team members receive access according to their roles and client assignments. Social networks receive the content and account requests you authorise when publishing is enabled. These providers may process information outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Workspace information is retained while needed to provide the service, maintain content history or meet applicable obligations. You may ask DarkMatter to remove your account, uploaded content or connected platform data. We will assess the request and explain any records that must be retained. Disconnecting a social account stops future access through that connection; you can also revoke the app in the social network's settings.

Your choices and contact

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact jason@darkm.co.za. Include your work email and the relevant account or client. Do not send passwords or access tokens.
